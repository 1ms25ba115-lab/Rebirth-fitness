5 beginner mistakes to avoid

Skipping warm-ups, ego lifting and ignoring sleep top the list. Start light, learn the form, and add weight slowly.

Eating well on an Indian diet

Dal, curd, paneer, eggs and millets make excellent protein sources. Aim for protein at every meal and keep portions balanced.

Mindset

Consistency beats intensity

Three sessions a week for a year beats thirty days of extremes. Build a routine you can keep during exams and busy months.
